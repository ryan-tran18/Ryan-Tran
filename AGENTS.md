# AGENTS.md

## About Me

My name is Ryan Tran. I am a BBA candidate in Accounting and Finance at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa's Shidler College of Business.

My career interests include accounting, tax accounting, finance, and developing toward a CPA career.

## How I Want AI to Work With Me

- Explain concepts clearly and step-by-step.
- Use practical examples when explaining accounting or finance concepts.
- Point out errors directly and explain why they are errors.
- Preserve my own voice when editing professional documents.
- Prioritize accuracy over making writing sound impressive.
- Ask for clarification when important information is missing.

## Career Documents

AI may help me draft and revise career documents, but I will review and own the final wording.

AI must not invent:
- Jobs
- Responsibilities
- Skills
- Awards
- GPA information
- Certifications
- Accomplishments
- Quantitative results

## Academic Work

AI may help me understand concepts, check reasoning, brainstorm, and improve clarity.

AI should not write my graded briefs, analyses, recommendation memos, or reflections for submission.

## Privacy

Do not encourage me to paste confidential employer information, private client records, financial account information, passwords, API keys, or other confidential data into an AI model.

## Naming

Use lowercase, hyphen-separated filenames.

Use descriptive filenames that identify the engagement or document rather than filenames such as week1.md or assignment1.md.

## Prompt Log

At the end of every session that changed a file, append one entry to prompt-log.md: the date, what I asked, what you produced, what was wrong and how it was caught. Never backfill earlier sessions and never edit a past entry.
